## Version 1.0

**Promotional text** (170, can change without a new build):

```
Snap a book's cover and copyright page and FirstFinder fills in the title, author, edition and printing. Track what you paid and what it's worth. Free, no ads.
```

**Description** (4000):

```
FirstFinder is a catalog for collectors: books first, and cards, comics, records, toys, posters and memorabilia too. Record what you own, what you paid and what it's worth, keep the photos and receipts with each item, and see the whole collection at a glance.

SNAP A PICTURE, SKIP THE TYPING
Photograph a book's cover and copyright page and FirstFinder reads the title, author, publisher and printing for you to check, and the edition too when the page states it. The printing comes from the number line, with the reason shown. Start typing a title instead and pick the book from the list to fill in the rest.

KNOW WHAT IT'S WORTH
Research a photo and FirstFinder searches what copies are actually listed for, then suggests the edition and a value range with its sources. It's a starting point, not an appraisal, and you review everything before it's saved. Photo research is limited to a few a day per account.

YOUR COLLECTION AT A GLANCE
The dashboard shows what your collection is worth, what you paid, how it has grown and your most recent finds, by category or by period. Browse as covers, cards or a list; search and filter by category, genre, edition and printing.

KEEP THE RECORD STRAIGHT
- Condition grades, editions and printings for every item
- Photos and receipts stored privately with your account
- Possible duplicates pointed out before you save
- Mark items sold and keep what they sold for
- One tap to look for similar copies on AbeBooks and eBay
- A printable report of your collection for insurance, or a CSV export

THE HUNT
Keep a wishlist of what you're looking for, the copy that would be a yes, and the most you'd pay. When you find it, photograph it and it moves into your collection.

SHARE IT, OR DON'T
Your collection is private. If you want, publish a read-only page at a link you control, choose what it shows, and keep prices off it. Browse other collectors' shelves on Explore.

FIRST-EDITION GUIDES
Sourced guides to telling a true first edition from a later printing, for books like Dune, The Great Gatsby, East of Eden and The Stand.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
FirstFinder is free, with no ads, and its code is public. It's made by one collector in their spare time. Light and dark themes, or follow your iPhone.

Works with the FirstFinder website: sign in with the same account and your collection is in both places.
```

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** Yes — the demo account's email and password go here.
**Contact:** your name, phone and email (only App Review sees these).

**Notes for the reviewer:**

```
FirstFinder is a catalog for collectors. Everything is behind an account; the demo account above has a sample collection already in it.

Things to try:
- Dashboard, Collection and Wishlist tabs: the sample collection, its value and the wishlist.
- Collection > any item: photos, receipt, value, Edit, Mark sold.
- Add > Quick add: type an item in and Submit, or "Snap a picture" of a book's copyright page to fill the form from the photo.
- Add > "Research a photo": identifies a book from a cover photo and suggests a value. Each account gets 2 of these a day, because each one is a paid call to an AI service; the remaining count is shown before the camera opens.
- More > Appearance: System, Light or Dark.

Account deletion: More > My Account > Delete my account. It permanently deletes the account, its records and its photos.

Sign in with Apple, Google and email/password are all supported.

Explore (More > Explore) shows collections whose owners chose to publish them.

The app is free, with no in-app purchases or ads.
```

## Age rating

Expected **4+**. Answer No to violence, profanity, mature themes, gambling,
medical, contests and unrestricted web access.

**Watch the user-generated content question.** Explore and shared collection
pages show other people's photos and notes. If App Store Connect asks whether
the app shows user-generated content, the honest answer is yes, and guideline
1.2 then expects a way to report objectionable content and to block it. Today
that is only the support email. See the open question in issue #279 before
answering.
