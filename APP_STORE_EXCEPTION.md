## Why this exists

deliver apps under digital rights management. The AGPL forbids passing on its
code under extra restrictions like those, which is why GPL-family apps have
been pulled from the App Store before. This permission lets FirstFinder ship
an iOS app while every line stays open source under the AGPL.
